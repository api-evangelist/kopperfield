# Kopperfield

> Professional software tools for electrical contractors and electricians. Create NEC-compliant load calculations, single line diagrams, and panel schedules with our free and paid tools.

Kopperfield provides web-based tools designed to simplify electrical design and permitting paperwork for residential and commercial projects. Our tools are used by electrical contractors, electricians, inspectors, and instructors.

Key features:
- Free electrical load calculator with automatic square footage detection
- Single line diagram creator with drag-and-drop templates
- Panel schedule generator with circuit directories
- NEC chatbot trained on 2017, 2020, and 2023 code editions
- AI-powered panel photo analysis
- Mobile app for iOS and Android

## Free Tools

- [Load Calculator](https://www.kopperfield.com/free-load-calculator): Create NEC-compliant electrical load calculations for residential and commercial projects
- [Single Line Diagram](https://www.kopperfield.com/single-line-diagram): Design professional electrical single line diagrams with pre-built templates
- [Panel Schedule](https://www.kopperfield.com/panel-schedule): Generate professional panel schedules with circuit directories
- [NEC Chatbot](https://www.kopperfield.com/national-electrical-code-online): Ask questions about the National Electrical Code (2017, 2020, 2023 editions)
